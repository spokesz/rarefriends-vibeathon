# ❄️ Play'n with Snow — Rare Friends Winter Wonderland & Santa's Sleigh

> An interactive 2D winter wonderland minigame built for the **Rare Friends Vibeathon**.

### ✨ Features
- ⛄ Parabolic snowball throwing physics & snow footprint trails
- 🎨 3-tier modular Snowman Builder studio
- 🎅 Santa Claus station with animated Santa NPC & interactive sounds
- 🛷 Wearable Santa Suit & Flying Reindeer Sleigh flyby across the sky
- 🎯 Snowball Target Range with procedural chimes
- 🪙 Rare Friends ($RF) rewards & Frost Crate mechanics
