# Rare Hands

**Builder / contact:** Diorblack ([@innocentblack66](https://x.com/innocentblack66))  
**Category:** Token Activity  
**SDK:** FriendSDK v0.1.2

A Rock Paper Scissors game where you play as your own Rare Friend and spend simulated $RAREFRIENDS (RF) to enter matches and claim rewards.

## Playable preview

https://innocent677.github.io/rare-hands/

Connect a browser wallet on **Robinhood mainnet (chain 4663)** that holds a hardwired Rare Friends Generations NFT (generation 1 or higher).  
All balances, purchases and rewards are **simulated**. No real transactions are sent.

## Source repository

https://github.com/innocent677/rare-hands

## How to play

1. Connect your wallet and select your Rare Friend.
2. Spend simulated RF to enter a Rock Paper Scissors match.
3. Choose Rock, Paper or Scissors.
4. Win to earn RF rewards. Losses burn the entry fee.
5. Play again and climb the score / reward ladder.

Controls work with mouse/touch and keyboard.

## Game rules & economy (simulated)

- Entry fee is paid in simulated RF.
- Wins return rewards in simulated RF.
- All balances, purchases, wins and burns are fully simulated.
- The game uses the FriendSDK ownership gate — only a verified owned Generations NFT can play.
- No real $RAREFRIENDS is spent or transferred.
